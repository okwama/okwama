I design and build the systems that sit underneath real operations: payment rails, ledgers, tax compliance, clinical records, freight and delivery. They're built for East Africa from the start, so M-Pesa, PesaLink, KRA eTIMS and local regulation are first-class concerns, not afterthoughts.

## Payments & Fintech

### Orbit · *CIT Logistics Systems*
Multi-tenant payments platform on Azure, plus an Android POS app for card terminals. It routes card and M-Pesa payments through DPO and bank-to-bank "Lipa na Bank" payments over PesaLink (ISO 20022 pacs.008 / pacs.002 / pacs.028), with split payments, dual receipts and partner sales integration.
`.NET` `Azure` `Kotlin` `Microservices` `PesaLink`

### InkBridge
Remote deposit capture platform aligned to the Central Bank of Kenya's cheque truncation system. 12-module system specification.
`Go` `Python`

### Africa Freelance Payout Card
USD wallets, virtual cards and local withdrawals for African freelancers, with identity verification built in.
`Stripe` `Flutterwave` `Smile ID`

## 📊 Accounting & ERP

### Clarity Books
Multi-tenant cloud accounting for East African SMEs: double-entry bookkeeping, PAYE, multi-warehouse inventory (FIFO / weighted average), fixed assets with automated depreciation, and direct KRA eTIMS (OSCU) integration.
`SvelteKit` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL`

### [GizmoJunction](https://www.gizmojunction.com/) · *Live*
E-commerce and retail ERP: eTIMS-compliant invoicing, M-Pesa checkout, AI product categorisation, and catalogue sync to Google Merchant Center and Jumia.
`SvelteKit` `Supabase` `Edge Functions` `Gemini`

## 🚚 Logistics & Mobility

### Shukisha · *TransitNode*
Transit and parcel delivery platform: event-driven backend, GPS telemetry at scale, M-Pesa B2C driver payouts, and an IFRS 15-compliant ledger with immutable journal entries.
`Go` `NATS / Kafka` `TimescaleDB` `PostgreSQL`

### Belmur Freight Logistics
Freight forwarding platform for East African perishables: public site, operations admin and a REST API covering shipments, air waybills and shipper/consignee workflows.
`SvelteKit` `PHP 8.2` `MySQL`

### [AirLogix](https://github.com/okwama/airlogix-android)
Private aviation and cargo booking platform, plus a native Android ground-handling app.
`Flutter` `Kotlin` `Jetpack Compose` `SvelteKit` `PHP`
